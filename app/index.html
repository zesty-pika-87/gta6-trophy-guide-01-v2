Leonida Platinum Roadbook

All 55 official trophies with a cited guide for each, a roadmap to the platinum with the full 100% checklist, one missable flagged — and a checklist that remembers where you stopped.

    Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10, 0 online

    Three stages, built from the official descriptions and the PowerPyx roadmap (figures cross-checked against AlcastHQ). No online trophies, no mission replay — save by hand at every chapter start. Free-roam stays open after the story, and everything on the 100% checklist is still reachable then.

    Story trophies by chapter — which mission, and when

    17 story trophies: 12 mission and chapter trophies, plus 5 contact strands that finish across the story. Chapter trophies pop as the story plays; the strands end when their last mission does.

    The 100% checklist — all 35 items (for A Long Road)

    The in-game checklist under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion, as PowerPyx and AlcastHQ list it: 12 story and task items, 20 exploration and activity items, 3 discovery items. Progress you make during the story already counts.

    Missables — one to watch, fifty-four that cannot be missed

    PowerPyx flags exactly one trophy as potentially missable. Everything else is safe: story missions, activities and collectibles all stay available in free-roam after the story ends, and there is no mission replay to lose.
